Heritage & Hospitality

Where Madurai Meets the World

Nestled along Madakulam Main Road within the distinguished ambiance of Regency Madurai by GRT Hotels, Ahaaram Multi Cuisine Restaurant celebrates the sacred bond between food, community, and generous hospitality.

From the bustling street kitchens of Madurai known for tender mutton kari dosas and fragrant Seeraga Samba dum biryanis, to the subtle nuances of continental herb grills and sizzlers, our kitchen crafts a welcoming sanctuary for every palate. Whether greeting the sunrise with our sumptuous breakfast buffet or gathering the family for an evening banquet, you will find an inviting table waiting.
